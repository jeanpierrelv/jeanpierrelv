### :man_technologist: About Me

- :teacher: **Substitute Professor** of Computer Architecture and Organization and Deep Learning at [São Paulo State University (UNESP)](https://igce.rc.unesp.br/#!/demac), Rio Claro, SP, Brazil.
- :microscope: **AI Research Collaborator** at the [Prisma Research Group](http://dgp.cnpq.br/dgp/espelhogrupo/9851379845566798) (DEMAC - UNESP) and at [VUNESP](https://www.vunesp.com.br/).
- :man_student: Ph.D. candidate in Electrical Engineering at the University of Campinas (UNICAMP). M.Sc. in Electrical Engineering from [UNICAMP](https://www.fee.unicamp.br/) and B.Sc. in Electronic Engineering from [San Luis Gonzaga National University](https://www.unica.edu.pe/fime/), Perú :peru:.
- :brain: My research focuses on **computer vision with deep learning**, **morphometric measurements from 3D tomographic images**, and **large language models**. I also work with time series forecasting, information retrieval, and control systems.
- :factory: Previously Project Manager / Automation Engineer at INDUCONTROL, Perú.
- :zap: In my free time, :muscle: I like to work out, :mountain_bicyclist: ride a bike, :tennis: play tennis, and :airplane: discover new places. :seedling: I'm always learning something new.
